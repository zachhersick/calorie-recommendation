Mifflin-St Jeor equation

A transparent daily calorie estimate

Enter age, sex, height, weight and activity level. The app estimates basal metabolic rate with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and scales it by a standard activity factor to estimate total daily energy needs. You can then log daily intake and compare it with that target.

Method
BMR × activity

The same equation dietitians commonly start from. No black box.

Accuracy
About ±10%

Individual needs vary. Treat the number as a starting point, not a prescription.
